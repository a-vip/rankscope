How RankScope scores keywords

Every number is explainable: open any keyword to see the exact inputs, their contribution and links to the raw evidence.

Demand

With DataForSEO connected, demand is real Google Ads monthly volume (log-scaled to 0-100: 100 searches = 40, 1,000 = 60, 10,000 = 80, 100,000 = 100). Without it, RankScope uses a relative demand index from autocomplete evidence: how many engines suggest the phrase, its rank in the suggestions, how many different prefixes trigger it, and Google's own suggestion relevance score. Autocomplete only suggests queries people type often, so this is a strong popularity signal, but it is relative and not a volume figure. Wikipedia pageviews provide real absolute interest figures at topic level.

Keyword difficulty (KD)

  • Measured (best): with a Serper.dev key, the live Google top 10 is analysed: position-weighted domain authority (Open PageRank if connected), how many results target the exact phrase in the title, SERP features that push organic results down, and how many slots are held by forums/UGC.
  • DataForSEO Labs KD is shown alongside when connected.
  • Estimated (fallback): word count, demand, intent, question/fresh/local modifiers and whether a dedicated Wikipedia article exists. Always labelled Low confidence.

Rankability (your chance of page 1)

KD compared with your site authority, adjusted for weak spots: low-authority pages already on page 1, forum/UGC results, outdated results and rising interest. Scale: Very likely, Likely, Possible, Unlikely, Very unlikely, Near-impossible (today). "Today" matters: authority grows as you publish and earn links.

Trend

Combines Google Trends "Trending now", Wikipedia pageview change (last 14 days vs prior 60), Google News velocity (last 7 days vs the prior 3-week baseline), GDELT global news volume and, with DataForSEO, 12 months of search volume. Labels: Breakout, Rising, Stable, Declining. Seasonality is detected from 3 years of monthly Wikipedia views.

Opportunity

38% demand + 42% ease (100 - KD) + 20% trend, blended 55/45 with rankability, plus a small bonus for long question queries and a penalty for navigational (someone else's brand) queries.

Honest limits

  • No tool can guarantee rankings. Google's algorithm uses hundreds of signals RankScope cannot see (your content quality, links, user engagement). Use the scores to choose battles you can win, then out-write the competition.
  • Absolute search volumes only come from Google Ads data (DataForSEO here). Tools that show volumes without such data are estimating.
  • Free sources are unofficial endpoints and may rate-limit or change. Source status is shown in Settings, and each source fails gracefully.
